The story

Comfort shoes shouldn't look like an apology.

Field & Form started with one shoe, photographed exactly where it belongs — outdoors, on real ground, not a studio backdrop.

Most business-casual shoes are designed for a desk and tolerated everywhere else. We built Slip-On No. 04 the other way around: a molded ribbed sole for grip and give, a soft leather-look upper that holds its shape, and a fit you can wear from a Tuesday commute to a Saturday errand without changing shoes twice.

No laces to catch. No break-in period to suffer through. Just a shoe that looks considered enough for the office and comfortable enough to forget you're wearing it.
